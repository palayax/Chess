Modifications from the original artwork (as required by CC BY-SA 3.0's "indicate changes"
term): the path geometry was hand-transcribed from SVG path data into Kotlin/Compose Path
draw calls and uniformly rescaled; a small number of purely decorative stroke-only accents
present in the originals (e.g. the queen's neckline arcs, the knight's nostril highlight) were
omitted; and stroke-only detail lines that are important for reading the piece (the bishop's
mitre slit and waist bands, the knight's eye) were reproduced as carved holes in the filled
silhouette rather than as separate colored strokes, to fit this app's single-path-per-piece
rendering model. The outer silhouette and proportions of each piece are otherwise unchanged
from the Cburnett originals.
