Palaya Chess

An Android app that reviews your chess games on your phone. Share a game (PGN) from chess.com, Lichess or anywhere else and get move grades from Brilliant to Blunder, the tactics each side found and missed, a walkthrough of the best line, practice positions from your own mistakes, and a narrated video review.

  • Analysis runs on your phone with the Stockfish 19 chess engine. No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • After a one-time download of the engine data and the narration voice (about 210 MB, only when you tap Download), the app works offline.
